# Budget records are source-agnostic

A Deposit, Spend or Refund looks the same whether someone typed it in or it came from outside Budgie. Records from outside live in their own tables, which are the bank transaction tables: bank transactions from a CSV, from bank sync and from Splitwise. A Splitwise expense doesn't get a table of its own, because it's a bank transaction of a Splitwise Account, whose signed amount is the person's net share of the expense ([ADR 0016](0016-splitwise-is-synced-as-an-account.md)). Filing one creates one or more ordinary budget records of any kind, each linked back to the external record it came from, or marks the external record as ignored (for example, a card payment between the user's own accounts). A Costco charge can be filed as a Spend from Groceries and another from Household, and a paycheck that includes reimbursed travel as a Deposit and a Refund. This keeps the core tables free of import columns, so they can be built before any importer exists. Undoing an import deletes the budget records it created, then its bank transactions.
