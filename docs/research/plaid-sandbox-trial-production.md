# Research: Plaid's Sandbox, Trial and Production for a Canadian team

Research for [#143](https://github.com/RobertG-H/budgie-src/issues/143), part of the map [#141](https://github.com/RobertG-H/budgie-src/issues/141) (Bank sync with Plaid).
Gathered 2026-11-19 from Plaid's own docs (plaid.com/docs, including the `.md` versions of its pages), its changelog, its institution pages, its Quickstart repo ([`plaid/quickstart`](https://github.com/plaid/quickstart) at [`d66d1a0`](https://github.com/plaid/quickstart/tree/d66d1a0413d778180055cc30cc9d19bb6bdad445), 2026-11-06), and Wealthsimple's help centre.
It builds on [bank-sync-aggregators.md](bank-sync-aggregators.md) (#37), which already covers Plaid's data model, `/transactions/sync`, pending to posted and the sign convention; none of that is repeated here.

**Question:** what can Budgie test without real banks, what does the Trial allow, and does Plaid cover Wealthsimple, CIBC and TD for transactions?

**Sources that couldn't be fetched directly.** Plaid's help centre (support.plaid.com) and Wealthsimple's help centre (help.wealthsimple.com) answer automated requests with 403. Where a claim rests on one of their articles it is marked *(search excerpt)*: it was read from a search engine's excerpt of that article, not the page itself, so it's lower trust than the rest. Every claim that could be is backed by plaid.com/docs instead.

## Summary

- **Coverage.** CIBC: Plaid's own institution page lists it for Transactions in Canada, for depository, credit, loan and investment accounts. TD Canada Trust: Plaid names it among its Canadian institutions, but no current public Plaid page lists its products. Wealthsimple: Plaid confirms Wealthsimple is a Plaid institution (its cash account for Auth, March 2025), but no Plaid or Wealthsimple page confirms Transactions for its Chequing (Cash) or credit card accounts. **None of the three can be confirmed for Transactions from a public, current Plaid source**; the authoritative check is `/institutions/search` or the Dashboard's Institutions page, which the operator can run with no Item and no cost. No Canadian bank uses OAuth, so none needs a redirect URI.
- **Sandbox** is free with unlimited Items. It can simulate **new** transactions (`/sandbox/transactions/create`, on any Sandbox Item since 2026-10-13, or `/transactions/refresh` on `user_transactions_dynamic`), **pending to posted** (`user_transactions_dynamic` plus `/transactions/refresh`, or a custom user with a future `date_posted`), and **login required** (`/sandbox/item/reset_login`, a custom user's `force_error`, and automatically after 30 days). It has **no documented way to modify or remove a posted transaction**, so those paths are only testable in the `PlaidFake`. Its one Canadian test bank is Tartan-Dominion Bank of Canada (`ins_43`).
- **The Trial** is free Production for teams created on or after 2026-04-15 in the US or Canada: 10 Items, ever. **Removing an Item does not free a slot.** Every Production access token counts, so an Item counts at the public-token exchange. It includes Transactions (and Transactions Refresh), Auth, Balance, Identity, Assets, Investments, Liabilities and Statements. Production Link needs a Data Transparency Messaging use case set in the Dashboard.
- **Leaving the Trial** is a Production access request from the Dashboard; it can't be undone. Prices for Transactions in Canada aren't published: they're shown on the request's last page. Trial Items stay connected, and every Trial Item with Transactions starts billing monthly, so remove throwaway Items first. Nothing built against the Trial changes: same host, same keys, same endpoints.
- **Fetching real transactions now:** the [Plaid CLI](https://plaid.com/docs/resources/cli/) (`plaid link --products transactions`, then `plaid transactions sync --json`) or the Quickstart, in Production. Each costs **one Trial Item per bank login, permanently**: 3 of 10 for Wealthsimple, CIBC and TD.

## Coverage: Wealthsimple, CIBC and TD

### How coverage is published

Plaid's public coverage explorer is US-only: its country selector lists only the United States and its CSV is `us_institution_coverage.csv`, and Plaid says "for the most up to date data, use /institutions/get or the Institutions page in the Dashboard" ([Institutions](https://plaid.com/docs/institutions/)). The per-bank marketing pages under plaid.com/institutions/ exist for some Canadian banks but are old (the search engine dates them about 3,100 days back).

`/institutions/search` takes a `query`, `country_codes` (`["CA"]`) and `products` ("Filter the Institutions based on whether they support all products listed in `products`"), and returns each institution's `products`, `oauth` ("Indicates that the institution has an OAuth login flow"), `connection_availability` (`SUPPORTED` or `NOT_SUPPORTED` for new Items) and, with `include_status`, `status.transactions_updates`, the health of transaction updates ([Institutions API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/institutions/)). US and Canada teams get US and Canadian institutions in Production and every country's in Sandbox (same page). It doesn't link anything, so it creates no Item and can't spend a Trial slot (an Item is created by exchanging a public token from Link; see [the Trial](#the-trial)).

### CIBC

Plaid's CIBC page lists Geography "CA", products "Assets, Auth, Balance, Transactions" and account types "Credit, Investment, Depository, Loan, Other" ([CIBC](https://plaid.com/institutions/cibc/)). The page is old, so treat it as likely rather than current; it says nothing about OAuth.

**Verdict:** covered for Transactions according to Plaid's own (dated) page, including credit cards. Confirm with `/institutions/search`.

### TD Canada Trust

Plaid's Canada launch post (2018-05-22, updated 2019-05-29) names "Royal Bank of Canada, Scotiabank, TD Canada Trust, Bank of Montreal, CIBC, and Tangerine" ([Plaid in Canada](https://plaid.com/blog/plaid-in-canada/)). The only current TD page, [TD Bank](https://plaid.com/institutions/td-bank/), is TD's **US** bank (Geography "US"); `plaid.com/institutions/td-canada-trust/` is a 404.

**Verdict:** TD Canada Trust is a Plaid institution, but **its Transactions support can't be confirmed from a public Plaid page**. Confirm with `/institutions/search`.

### Wealthsimple

- Plaid's March 2025 product update: "Auth now supports cash management accounts from Morgan Stanley, Betterment, and WealthSimple" ([Product Updates, March 2025](https://plaid.com/blog/product-updates-march-2025/)). That's Plaid confirming Wealthsimple is an institution, with Auth on its cash account; it says nothing about Transactions.
- Plaid has no institution page for it (`plaid.com/institutions/wealthsimple/` is a 404).
- Wealthsimple's help centre has an article on linking a **Business** chequing account to accounting software through Plaid, which needs "an active Wealthsimple Business chequing account" and "an accounting platform that supports Plaid integrations" ([Link your Business chequing account to accounting software](https://help.wealthsimple.com/hc/en-ca/articles/48319177129755-Link-your-Business-chequing-account-to-accounting-software), *search excerpt*). No Wealthsimple article found covers a **personal** Chequing (Cash) or credit card account being read by a third-party app through Plaid.
- Third-party budgeting apps say they read Wealthsimple through Plaid, but they aren't primary sources and aren't relied on here.

**Verdict:** Wealthsimple is a Plaid institution, but **Transactions for its Chequing (Cash) account and its credit card can't be confirmed from Plaid's or Wealthsimple's own pages**. Whether its credit card shows up as a separate account in the same Item is unknown. This needs a `/institutions/search` for "Wealthsimple" with `products: ["transactions"]` and, if it's listed, the first real Item (see [fetching real transactions](#fetching-real-transactions-before-budgie-can)) to see which accounts come back.

### OAuth and redirect URIs

"OAuth connections are not currently used by financial institutions in Canada" ([OAuth guide](https://plaid.com/docs/link/oauth/)), so Wealthsimple, CIBC and TD are credential-based Items. A `redirect_uri` is only for OAuth flows ("used to support OAuth authentication flows when launching Link in the browser or another app", [Link API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/link/)); the OAuth guide says to always set one for mobile web, and that it must be HTTPS except for localhost on Sandbox and registered under Allowed redirect URIs in the Dashboard ([OAuth guide](https://plaid.com/docs/link/oauth/)). For Canadian banks Budgie doesn't need one. `/institutions/search`'s `oauth` field is the per-bank check.

Link must be told about Canada: `country_codes` in `/link/token/create` takes `CA` (the Quickstart's `.env.example` sets `PLAID_COUNTRY_CODES=US,CA`, and "If Link is launched with multiple country codes, only products that you are enabled for in all countries will be used by Link", [`.env.example`](https://github.com/plaid/quickstart/blob/d66d1a0413d778180055cc30cc9d19bb6bdad445/.env.example)). Plaid's own table lists Transactions' supported countries as "US, CA, UK, Europe" ([Financial Insights](https://plaid.com/docs/financial-insights/)).

## Sandbox

"Usage of Sandbox is always free" ([Billing](https://plaid.com/docs/account/billing/)). Plaid's help centre says Sandbox has unlimited Items ([How are Sandbox, Production, Trial plan, and Limited Production different?](https://support.plaid.com/hc/en-us/articles/16110110883479-How-are-Sandbox-Production-Trial-plan-and-Limited-Production-different), *search excerpt*).

### Test credentials and users

| Username | Password | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `user_good` | `pass_good` | "The default username/password combination for all Sandbox institutions" ([Sandbox](https://plaid.com/docs/sandbox/)); basic access to most products ([Test credentials](https://plaid.com/docs/sandbox/test-credentials/)). MFA code `1234` ([Quickstart README](https://github.com/plaid/quickstart/blob/d66d1a0413d778180055cc30cc9d19bb6bdad445/README.md#test-credentials)). |
| `user_good` | `error_<CODE>`, such as `error_ITEM_LOCKED` | Simulates that error at Link; doesn't work with OAuth institutions ([Test credentials](https://plaid.com/docs/sandbox/test-credentials/)). |
| `user_transactions_dynamic` | any non-blank | Checking, credit card and loan accounts; "starts with 50 transactions per account in a mix of `pending` and `posted` states", plus recurring ones; "generates transaction updates on each call to /transactions/refresh" ([Transactions data](https://plaid.com/docs/transactions/transactions-data/), [Transactions](https://plaid.com/docs/transactions/)). Through interactive Link it needs "a non-OAuth test institution such as First Platypus Bank (`ins_109508`)". |
| `user_custom` | a JSON configuration | A custom user: chosen accounts and transactions ([below](#custom-users)). |

Persona users (`user_ewa_user`, `user_yuppie`, `user_small_business`) exist but their contents aren't described ([Test credentials](https://plaid.com/docs/sandbox/test-credentials/)).

### Canadian test institutions

The Sandbox institutions list has one labelled Canadian: **Tartan-Dominion Bank of Canada, `ins_43`** ("Canadian bank"), non-OAuth. The non-OAuth US ones Plaid steers Transactions tests to are First Platypus Bank (`ins_109508`) and First Gingham Credit Union (`ins_109509`); Unhealthy Platypus Bank - Degraded and - Down (`ins_132363`, `ins_132361`) and Unsupported Platypus Bank (`ins_133402`) simulate institution trouble ([Sandbox institutions](https://plaid.com/docs/sandbox/institutions/)). US/CA teams see every country's institutions in Sandbox ([Institutions API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/institutions/)), and `user_good` works at "all Sandbox institutions", but the docs don't say whether a real Canadian bank's Sandbox entry behaves like it does in Production, so Sandbox tells Budgie nothing about Wealthsimple, CIBC or TD.

### Custom users

A custom user is made in the Dashboard's **Sandbox Studio** (Build → Sandbox: describe the user in plain language, or edit the JSON) or by passing `user_custom` and the JSON as the password, most often through `/sandbox/public_token/create`'s `options.override_username` and `override_password` ([Custom users](https://plaid.com/docs/sandbox/user-custom/)). The configuration has accounts (`type`, `subtype`, `starting_balance`, `currency`) and transactions (`date_transacted`, `date_posted`, `amount`, `description`, `currency`). "Posted dates in the future will result in pending transactions." Limits: about 55 kB or 250 transactions, at most ten accounts, and custom users "may not work properly" at OAuth institutions. `force_error` sets an error at Item creation, including `ITEM_LOGIN_REQUIRED` and `ITEM_LOCKED`. "Editing a saved custom user doesn't change Items already created with it" (all from [Custom users](https://plaid.com/docs/sandbox/user-custom/)). Sandbox Studio launched 2026-09-11 ([Changelog](https://plaid.com/docs/changelog/)).

`/sandbox/public_token/create` makes a Sandbox Item without Link, for an `institution_id` and `initial_products`, with `options.transactions.days_requested` (1 to 730, default 90) ([Sandbox API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/sandbox/)). That's the way to make Items in specs-adjacent scripts and on the testing host without clicking through Link.

### The simulators, against what Budgie needs

| Budgie needs to see | How in Sandbox | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New** posted transactions | `/sandbox/transactions/create`: up to 10 per call, with `date_transacted`, `date_posted` (today or up to 730 days back, never future, so always posted), `amount` ("Can be negative"), `description`, optional `iso_currency_code` (**defaults to USD**) and `account_id`. Works "with any Item created in the Sandbox environment" (since 2026-10-13; before that only `user_transactions_dynamic`), and added ones persist across `/transactions/sync`. Or `/transactions/refresh` on `user_transactions_dynamic`, which "generates new `pending` transactions ... and adds any newly scheduled recurring transactions". | [Sandbox API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/sandbox/), [Changelog](https://plaid.com/docs/changelog/), [Transactions data](https://plaid.com/docs/transactions/transactions-data/) |
| **Pending → posted** | `user_transactions_dynamic` plus `/transactions/refresh`, which "moves previously `pending` transactions to `posted`" and fires the webhooks. Or a custom user whose transaction has a future `date_posted`: it "posts on the first transactions update on or after that date, such as one you trigger with /transactions/refresh". Per #37, sync shows this as the pending id in `removed` and a new posted one in `added`. | [Transactions data](https://plaid.com/docs/transactions/transactions-data/) |
| **Changed** (a posted transaction `modified`) | **No documented simulator.** No Sandbox endpoint edits a transaction, and editing a custom user doesn't change existing Items. | [Sandbox API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/sandbox/), [Custom users](https://plaid.com/docs/sandbox/user-custom/) |
| **Removed** (a posted transaction in `removed`) | **No documented simulator.** Only a pending one's removal, as part of posting. | same |
| **Login required** | `/sandbox/item/reset_login` "forces an Item into an `ITEM_LOGIN_REQUIRED` state"; Link in update mode restores it. Sandbox Items "will transition to an `ITEM_LOGIN_REQUIRED` error state automatically after 30 days" whether or not it's called. A custom user's `force_error: "ITEM_LOGIN_REQUIRED"` fails at creation instead. | [Sandbox API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/sandbox/), [Custom users](https://plaid.com/docs/sandbox/user-custom/) |
| **Initial history not ready yet** | Not simulated as such; `/transactions/sync` returns empty arrays with `transactions_update_status` `NOT_READY` until the first pull is done, in any environment. | [Transactions API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/products/transactions/) |
| Webhooks | `/sandbox/item/fire_webhook` fires `SYNC_UPDATES_AVAILABLE` or `DEFAULT_UPDATE` (with `options.new_transactions`); webhooks also "fire in Sandbox under the same conditions that they would in Production". Out of scope for Budgie, which polls. | [Sandbox API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/sandbox/) |

`/transactions/refresh` is a paid add-on in Production ("billed on a per-request model", [Transactions](https://plaid.com/docs/transactions/)), included in the Trial ([Billing](https://plaid.com/docs/account/billing/)), and free in Sandbox like everything else. In Production it isn't needed: Plaid checks for new transactions "one to four times per day" ([Transactions webhooks](https://plaid.com/docs/transactions/webhooks/)).

## The Trial

From [Pricing and billing](https://plaid.com/docs/account/billing/) unless marked:

- **Who:** "Free Trial plans are available to new Plaid teams (US/Canada only) created on or after April 15, 2026." Trial plans "replace Limited Production for all newly created Plaid teams going forward" and "use a streamlined approval questionnaire" ([Changelog, 2026-04-16](https://plaid.com/docs/changelog/)). Plaid's help centre adds that it's for teams without an existing Production or Limited Production account, needs the Master Services Agreement accepted, and is mostly approved automatically after identity verification ([What is the Plaid Trial plan?](https://support.plaid.com/hc/en-us/articles/39994173227159-What-is-the-Plaid-Trial-plan), *search excerpt*).
- **The limit:** "You can create 10 Production Items on a Trial plan." "After creating 10 Items in Production, you must upgrade to a paid plan to create more Production Items."
- **Removing doesn't free a slot:** "Removing Items created on a Trial plan (using /item/remove) will not allow you to create more Items." The API reference says the same: "On a Trial plan, calling /item/remove does not impact the number of remaining Trial Items" ([Items API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/items/)).
- **What counts as an Item:** "All access tokens created in Production will count against your Trial plan Item limit." An Item is "a login at a financial institution" ([Glossary](https://plaid.com/docs/quickstart/glossary/)), holding all the accounts that login sees, so one Wealthsimple login with Chequing and a credit card is one Item. Since the count is of access tokens, a Link session whose public token is never exchanged doesn't count, which is why Plaid's duplicate-Item guidance says to compare the institution and accounts in `onSuccess` and, on a match, not exchange the token ([Duplicate Items](https://plaid.com/docs/link/duplicate-items/)). Linking the same credentials twice makes two Items. Repairing an Item with Link in **update mode** keeps the same Item and access token ("An Item's `access_token` does not change when using Link in update mode", [Update mode](https://plaid.com/docs/link/update-mode/)), so a reconnect costs no slot, but a fresh link does.
- **Products:** "Assets, Auth, Balance, Identity, Investments (including Investments Refresh), Liabilities, Transactions (including Transactions Refresh), Statements."
- **Countries:** the team must be in the US or Canada. Plaid's help centre says US and Canadian institutions are available on the Trial and others aren't, and Trial teams have had "immediate access to all OAuth institutions" since 2026-05-20 ([Changelog](https://plaid.com/docs/changelog/); Trial article, *search excerpt*). Trial users can defer the OAuth registration requirements until they upgrade ([OAuth guide](https://plaid.com/docs/link/oauth/)); irrelevant for Canadian banks.
- **Data-use and display terms:** no Trial-specific terms were found. What applies is Production's: "New customers launching in the US and/or Canada must select a use case to use Link in Production" (Data Transparency Messaging, chosen in the Dashboard's Link customization, up to three use cases), and Canadian sessions show DTM ([DTM guide](https://plaid.com/docs/link/data-transparency-messaging-migration-guide/)). The Quickstart's notes say the same: "To use Production, you must set a use case for Link" ([`.env.example`](https://github.com/plaid/quickstart/blob/d66d1a0413d778180055cc30cc9d19bb6bdad445/.env.example)). The Trial also involves accepting Plaid's Master Services Agreement (*search excerpt*, above), whose terms weren't reviewed here.
- **Products are fixed per Item:** "a product cannot be removed from an Item once the Item has been initialized with that product", and "In Production, you will be billed for each product that you specify when initializing Link" ([Link API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/link/)). `transactions.days_requested` (default 90, max 730) can't be changed once Transactions is on the Item (same page).

## Moving from the Trial to full Production

- **The request:** made in the Dashboard (Build → Keys, or the "Get Production access" banner); it asks for business details, products, beneficial ownership and a plan (Pay-as-you-go with no minimum, Growth with a 12-month commitment, or Custom through sales). "Once you submit a Production access application, you cannot revert to the Trial plan." The security questionnaire unlocks after the application is approved ([How do I upgrade from the Trial plan to a paid Production plan?](https://support.plaid.com/hc/en-us/articles/39917307426967-How-do-I-upgrade-from-the-Trial-plan-to-a-paid-Production-plan), *search excerpt*). The Plaid CLI's `plaid trial` opens the Trial onboarding, then `plaid keys fetch` refreshes the keys ([CLI](https://plaid.com/docs/resources/cli/)). The old Launch Checklist "has been replaced by the Launch Center" in the Dashboard ([Launch checklist](https://plaid.com/docs/launch-checklist/)).
- **Pricing for Transactions in Canada isn't published.** The pricing page shows plans with no per-Item prices ([Pricing](https://plaid.com/pricing/)); "Pricing information for Pay-as-you-go and Growth plans will be displayed on the last page before you submit your request" ([Billing](https://plaid.com/docs/account/billing/)). The model is known: Transactions is a subscription, "an Item will incur a monthly subscription fee as long as a valid access_token exists for the Item", the cycle is calendar months in UTC, and "Fees for Items created or removed in the middle of the month are not pro-rated." `/item/remove` "is required to end subscription billing for the Item" ([Items API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/items/)). Pay-as-you-go is "most appropriate for hobbyist use" (#37, from [Billing](https://plaid.com/docs/account/billing/)).
- **What carries over:** "If you added a subscription fee product (such as Investments, Liabilities, or Transactions) to an Item during your trial, upon upgrading to a paid plan, you will begin to be charged for that subscription", and "you will only retain Production access to any products you explicitly requested" ([Billing](https://plaid.com/docs/account/billing/)). The help centre says existing Trial Items stay connected (*search excerpt*, upgrade article above).
- **Does anything built against the Trial change?** Nothing in the docs suggests so: the Trial *is* Production, "Using a Trial plan, you can use Plaid with real production data for free" ([Billing](https://plaid.com/docs/account/billing/)), with the same Production host and keys (the CLI and the Quickstart only distinguish `sandbox` and `production`, [CLI](https://plaid.com/docs/resources/cli/), [`.env.example`](https://github.com/plaid/quickstart/blob/d66d1a0413d778180055cc30cc9d19bb6bdad445/.env.example)). What changes is the limit (gone) and the bill (every live Transactions Item, from the first month after upgrading). The only hard error on the Trial is creating an 11th Item.

## Fetching real transactions before Budgie can

Every option below links a real bank in Production, so **each bank login costs one of the 10 Trial Items, permanently** (removing it later stops billing on a paid plan but never frees the slot). Wealthsimple, CIBC and TD are 3 Items, leaving 7 for Budgie. Before spending any, check coverage for free with `/institutions/search` or the Dashboard's Institutions page ([Institutions](https://plaid.com/docs/institutions/)): it makes no Item.

**The Dashboard** can't do it: no Plaid doc describes linking a bank or viewing an Item's transactions from the Dashboard. It has activity logs ([Quickstart README, troubleshooting](https://github.com/plaid/quickstart/blob/d66d1a0413d778180055cc30cc9d19bb6bdad445/README.md#cant-get-a-link-token-or-api-calls-are-400ing)) and the Sandbox Studio, nothing for reading a real Item.

**The Plaid CLI (recommended).** First-party, installed with `brew install plaid/plaid-cli/plaid`, and it covers Transactions. `plaid link --products transactions` opens Link in the browser and saves the access token and Item id to the CLI's own local config; `plaid transactions sync` syncs "transactions incrementally" and `plaid transactions list` lists recent ones; "All commands support the `--json` flag"; `plaid item list` and `plaid item remove` manage Items; `plaid config set --env production` switches environment, and each environment keeps its own credentials ([CLI](https://plaid.com/docs/resources/cli/)). Gaps in its docs: no flag is documented for `country_codes` or `days_requested`, and the sync command's output fields aren't described, so check `plaid link --help` before linking (if Canadian banks don't appear, or it can't ask for more than 90 days and the comparison needs more, use the Quickstart or a script instead). The CLI ships only as a Homebrew tap ([`plaid/homebrew-plaid-cli`](https://github.com/plaid/homebrew-plaid-cli)); its source isn't public.

**The Quickstart.** Clone [`plaid/quickstart`](https://github.com/plaid/quickstart), set `.env`: `PLAID_ENV=production`, `PLAID_PRODUCTS=transactions` (the default is `auth,transactions,signal`, and "When moving to Production, make sure to update this list with only the products you plan to use. Otherwise, you may be billed for unneeded products"), `PLAID_COUNTRY_CODES=CA`, and leave `PLAID_REDIRECT_URI` empty; set a Link use case in the Dashboard first ([`.env.example`](https://github.com/plaid/quickstart/blob/d66d1a0413d778180055cc30cc9d19bb6bdad445/.env.example)). Its Transactions button pages `/transactions/sync` from an empty cursor, prints every page to the backend's console and shows only "the 8 most recent transactions" in the browser; the access token lives in a variable in memory ([`python/server.py`](https://github.com/plaid/quickstart/blob/d66d1a0413d778180055cc30cc9d19bb6bdad445/python/server.py#L381-L417)), so restarting the backend loses it while the Item, and its slot, stay. It also uses the default 90 days of history.

**A small script** against the API gives full control (`transactions.days_requested` up to 730 in `/link/token/create`, then exchange, then `/transactions/sync` until `has_more` is false, [Link API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/link/), [Transactions API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/products/transactions/)) but still needs a browser page for Link, which is most of the Quickstart's work.

Whichever is used: the output is real bank data and the access tokens are secrets, so both stay out of the repo; `/transactions/sync` may return empty with `NOT_READY` for a while after linking ([Transactions API](https://plaid.com/docs/api/products/transactions/)); and `days_requested` is fixed when the Item is made, so if those Items might later be handed to Budgie rather than linked again, decide the history wanted (up to 730 days) before linking.

## Implications for Budgie

1. **Coverage is unconfirmed for all three; check before building or linking.** The operator runs `/institutions/search` (or the Dashboard's Institutions page) for Wealthsimple, CIBC and TD with `country_codes: ["CA"]` and `products: ["transactions"]`: free, no Item. Record `products`, `oauth` and `connection_availability` in `docs/plaid.md`. If Wealthsimple isn't listed for Transactions, it stays CSV-only under the map's "Plaid only" rule.
2. **No redirect URI for the three banks**, since no Canadian bank uses OAuth. Budgie's Link page still needs `country_codes: ["CA"]`, `products: ["transactions"]` only (products can't be removed and each is billed after the Trial), and a deliberate `days_requested`, which is fixed per Item.
3. **Count Items at the public-token exchange.** The Trial counts access tokens, not live Items, so Budgie's cap is "Items ever created in Production", which `/item/remove` never lowers. Budgie should check `onSuccess` metadata against existing connections and not exchange a duplicate, and reconnect through update mode (same Item, same token), never a fresh link. The operator's comparison Items count too: 3 used leaves 7.
4. **The `PlaidFake` is the only place modified and removed posted transactions are tested.** Sandbox covers new transactions, pending to posted (which Budgie should ignore: the pending id in `removed` was never stored) and login required. A testing-host check of "changed" or "removed" isn't possible.
5. **The testing host's Sandbox Item breaks every 30 days** (`ITEM_LOGIN_REQUIRED`), which conveniently exercises needs-reconnect and update mode; Sandbox Items are free and unlimited, so a new one is fine too.
6. **Make Sandbox data CAD.** `/sandbox/transactions/create` defaults to USD and the docs don't say what currency `user_transactions_dynamic` uses, while Budgie refuses another currency at link time. Use a custom user with `currency: "CAD"` accounts (Tartan-Dominion Bank of Canada, `ins_43`, or First Platypus) and pass `iso_currency_code: "CAD"` when adding transactions. Whether `user_transactions_dynamic` can be CAD is an open question for the first Sandbox call.
7. **Production needs a Link use case** (Data Transparency Messaging) set in the Dashboard before Budgie's Link works there: an item for `docs/plaid.md`'s manual ops.
8. **Upgrading later changes only the cap and the bill.** No code path differs between the Trial and paid Production. Before upgrading, remove the comparison Items and any other unused ones, since each Trial Item with Transactions starts billing monthly from the upgrade, unprorated.
